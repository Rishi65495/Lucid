How goals and achievements differ

Goals are targets you choose.

Examples: finish a resource, practise 30 minutes daily, or reach a mastery target. Lucid tracks the goal status and deadline.

Achievements are milestones you earn.

Badges are awarded when recorded learning evidence meets a defined rule. They are not goals and cannot be manually checked off.
